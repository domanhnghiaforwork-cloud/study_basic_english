# 93. The Puppet That Would Not Blink

We design a puppet to **amuse** children at the library. Its eyes should **blink**, and its mouth should pretend to **bite** a wooden apple. Instead, a loose string makes the head **bend** and **block** the view. We **analyze** the problem while a friend tries to **attract** the waiting children with a song. One loud laugh seems to **awaken** the smallest visitor, who begins to cry. We **apologize** and decide to **ban** sudden noises from the show. The puppet returns the next week, moving slowly enough for everyone to enjoy.
