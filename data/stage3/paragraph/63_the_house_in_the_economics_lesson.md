# 63. The House in the Economics Lesson

Our economics teacher invents a family deciding whether to **rent** a house. Each group calculates a **fee**, compares a **mortgage**, and discusses the **property** as an **asset**. A second card introduces a **liability** and asks us to reconsider. Later, the lesson moves from family **equity** to a company's **capitalization**, with **inflation** and **deflation** changing the background. None of our first answers survives every new condition. We finish by drawing question marks around the house, a reminder that understanding a model includes noticing what it leaves out.
