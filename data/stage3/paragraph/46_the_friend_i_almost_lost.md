# 46. The Friend I Almost Lost

When my best friend wins a writing prize, **jealousy** catches me by surprise. My **envy** turns into **anger**, and I say something unkind. The **joy** on her face disappears. Later, **loneliness** fills the walk home, and **sadness** makes it difficult to write an apology. I do not want one bad moment to end our **friendship**. She listens when I return and offers **forgiveness**, but we both need time. Her **compassion** teaches me something that the prize never could: caring for someone includes admitting the **sorrow** I caused.
