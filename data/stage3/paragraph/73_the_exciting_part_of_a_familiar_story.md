# 73. The Exciting Part of a Familiar Story

I feel **excited** when our theater group chooses a **familiar** story. The director promises an **exciting** version, but she refuses to rush the **essential** preparation. We practice again, taking turns so the process stays **fair**. By opening night, nobody expects a perfect show. We simply want the audience to enjoy a **fantastic** evening together.
