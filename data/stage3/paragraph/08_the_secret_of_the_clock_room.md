# 08. The Secret of the Clock Room

Restoring the station clock becomes our club's shared **purpose**. Each member has a **role**, and every tool has a safety **rule**. At first, the **process** seems slow, but photographs show our **progress**. My **responsibility** is to label the pieces, a task with less **risk** than climbing the tower. Working beside my grandfather changes our **relationship**. We talk more freely while our hands are busy. His **secret** is simple: do one careful thing at a time. By autumn, the clock gives the whole street a new **sense** of rhythm.
