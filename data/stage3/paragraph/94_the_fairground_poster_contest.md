# 94. The Fairground Poster Contest

A sudden gust threatens to **blow** every sketch off the table as our poster contest begins. We **chase** the papers and **calculate** how many are missing. One student uses a **brush** to flatten a damp page, while another tries to **capture** the whole scene in a photograph. We **classify** the entries by age and remind everyone not to **cheat**. During judging, younger children **clap** and **cheer** for every picture. The old display stand looks ready to collapse, so we move it away. Nobody lets one accident spoil the afternoon; the winning picture shows a dragon that can **burn** a castle.
