# 95. A Bridge in the Bicycle Game

We **compete** to **construct** a bridge inside a computer game. Our riders must **cycle** across before a storm can **damage** the supports. The first design begins to **collapse**, and a cartoon **crash** sends everyone back to the start. We **convert** spare parts into a stronger base, but the repairs **consume** most of our imaginary budget. The game cannot **cure** our habit of rushing. It can show us the consequences. By the last attempt, we check every support before letting a single rider **cross** the river.
