# 54. The Poster with Too Many Names

Our school **principal** asks for a poster about next month's arts and science evening. A **writer** and a **poet** will read, an **actress** will perform, and a **director** will answer questions. An **artist** will display sketches beside a **scientist** and a **researcher** showing an experiment. A **professor** and a **lecturer** will lead the final discussion. I cannot fit every name on one page. Then I draw a long table instead of separate boxes, and the poster begins to show people meeting rather than subjects competing for space.
