# 40. Words for the Waiting Room

A fictional clinic asks our design class to make its waiting room easier to understand. We begin with words such as **illness**, **disease**, **infection**, and **symptom**. A poster comparing a **virus** with **bacteria** takes several drafts because our teacher wants us to check every label. Another display explains that **diagnosis** and **treatment** belong in conversations with trained staff. We place **mineral** and **calorie** on a separate nutrition board. The project teaches me that a clear sentence can help, while an unclear one can leave people with more questions.
