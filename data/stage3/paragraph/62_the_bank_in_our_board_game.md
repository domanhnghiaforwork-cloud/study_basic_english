# 62. The Bank in Our Board Game

We add a bank to our class board game. Every **payment** becomes a **transaction** written on a card. Players can make a **deposit**, request a **withdrawal**, or arrange a **transfer** between accounts. One player takes a **loan**, then discovers that **debt** affects the next turn. Another forgets to include **tax** in her **expenditure**. We change the interest **rate** to see how the game responds. By the final round, nobody is rushing. The imaginary money has given us real reasons to read instructions and check our calculations.
