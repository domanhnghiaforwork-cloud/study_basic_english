# 42. The Characters in the Clinic Play

Our drama club writes a play set in a crowded clinic. One character arrives with a **headache**, another with a **stomachache**, and a third complains of **toothache**. An older visitor wants to discuss **diabetes**, while a woman is waiting for news about **cancer**. A child with **asthma** sits beside his father. My character has a **sore** hand, a **swollen** finger, and a **bleeding** **wound** drawn with washable paint. We revise the scene carefully so the people sound like individuals with different worries, rather than a list of medical words.
