# 01. Opening the Workshop Doors

The town workshop used to open only during school hours. Giving evening **access** to everyone became our club's greatest **achievement**. At first, an **argument** divided the members: some feared noisy **behavior**, while others saw an **advantage** for working parents. We reached an **agreement** after a simple **analysis** of attendance. A small **amount** of money paid for better lights. More important was a change in **attitude**. Volunteers began paying **attention** to what visitors needed, and the workshop slowly became a place where the whole neighborhood could learn.
