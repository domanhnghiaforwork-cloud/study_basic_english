# 100. The Winter Show on Wheels

Our winter show combines a sewing workshop with a scene about learning to **skate**. We **select** bright cloth, **sew** small flags, and **roll** them into a basket. Onstage, children **skip** between paper snowflakes that **scatter** when the fan starts. One actor must pretend to **slip**, **rub** his knee, and **scream** in surprise. We change the movement so it cannot **scratch** the floor or frighten the youngest guests. By evening, the show looks effortless. Only the performers know how many careful choices lie behind one minute of cheerful confusion.
