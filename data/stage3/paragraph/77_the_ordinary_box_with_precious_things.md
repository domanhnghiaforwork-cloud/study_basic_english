# 77. The Ordinary Box with Precious Things

An **ordinary** box on Grandma's shelf holds her most **precious** things. There is an **original** drawing, a **personal** letter, and a photograph from a **pleasant** afternoon. None is **perfect**, but each has a **powerful** place in her memory. I offer a **practical** idea: make copies for a more **permanent** record. She likes the suggestion, and we spend a **positive** afternoon naming the people in every picture. By evening, the box is back on the shelf. It looks the same, though I can no longer walk past without noticing it.
