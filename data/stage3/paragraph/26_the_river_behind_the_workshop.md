# 26. The River behind the Workshop

A small **fire** in a fictional workshop leaves **ash** beside the river. Nobody is injured, but a child notices one **flame** reflected in dirty water. The story becomes a class project about **pollution**. We make separate labels for **wastewater**, **garbage**, **trash**, and **rubbish**, then discuss how some words overlap. Our model town adds a **recycling** station and treats water as a shared **resource**. The final scene shows neighbors cleaning the riverbank together. It is an imagined town, but the questions about responsibility feel close to home.
