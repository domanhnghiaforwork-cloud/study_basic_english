# 35. The Tiny Museum in Our Garden

My brother calls our garden a museum after finding an empty **shell** and a small **lizard**. We watch a **butterfly** near a flower while a **bee** passes overhead. An **ant** carries something larger than itself, and a **spider** waits beneath a leaf. The only **insect** nobody welcomes is a **mosquito**. A **worm** appears when Dad moves a pot. My brother wants a **crocodile** for the pond, but Dad laughs and gives him a toy one. The garden already has enough surprises without adding a dangerous visitor.
