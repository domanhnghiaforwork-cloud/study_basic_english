# 51. The Empty Shop on the Corner

An **entrepreneur** wants to turn the empty corner shop into a repair market. A **banker** discusses the plan with her, while an **investor** asks how it will grow. Her **assistant** invites a **merchant**, a **salesperson**, and a local **consultant** to an open afternoon. There, each **seller** meets a possible **buyer**, and a volunteer **cashier** practices using the till. Nothing is sold yet, but people leave their names. By evening, the shop is still empty of products and full of possibilities that did not exist that morning.
