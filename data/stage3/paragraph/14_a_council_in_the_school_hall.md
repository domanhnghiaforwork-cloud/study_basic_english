# 14. A Council in the School Hall

Our class invents a **government** for a school play. One group studies a **parliament**, while another writes scenes about a **congress** in a different fictional country. The **president** and a **minister** disagree over a broken bridge. Their **political** argument becomes the central problem of the story. Our teacher reminds us that **politics** affects **human** lives, so the villagers need voices too. A quiet student becomes their **leader** and asks everyone to listen. Her **leadership** changes the ending: the bridge is repaired after the characters finally work together.
