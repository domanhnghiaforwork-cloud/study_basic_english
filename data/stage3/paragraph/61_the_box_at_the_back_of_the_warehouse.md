# 61. The Box at the Back of the Warehouse

During a **warehouse** visit, we trace a missing **shipment** through the **inventory**. It appears in the **stock** list, but nobody can find it. A **shareholder** visiting that morning asks about the **loss**, while the manager worries about the **expense** of sending another box. An **investment** in better labels suddenly seems less boring. Before lunch, a worker finds the parcel behind a display of old packaging. It will not add much **revenue** or **profit**, but its journey gives our class a useful story about checking small details.
