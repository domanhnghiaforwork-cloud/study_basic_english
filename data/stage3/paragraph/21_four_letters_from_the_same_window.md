# 21. Four Letters from the Same Window

My cousin sends four letters describing the view from her window. In **summer**, the **sun** fills her desk with **sunshine**. In **autumn**, a low **cloud** covers the hill before the **rain** begins. By **winter**, she can see the **moon** earlier, and one bright **star** appears above the roof. I pin her letters beside my own window and compare the **sky** in our two towns. We live far apart, but watching the same evening become dark makes the distance feel a little smaller.
