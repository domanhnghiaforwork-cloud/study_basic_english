# 27. Power for the Little Lighthouse

Our model lighthouse needs **power**, so the science club compares **fuel**, **electricity**, and a **battery**. We add a small **solar** panel and record what happens on a **sunny** afternoon. A **cloudy** day brings different results, while **rainy** and **stormy** weather keep the model indoors. On a **windy** morning, we test a tiny paper windmill instead. The experiment does not give us one perfect answer. It gives us a notebook full of observations and a better reason to ask what each kind of equipment can do.
