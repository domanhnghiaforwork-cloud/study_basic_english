# 34. The Animal Rescue Board Game

We design a board game about returning lost animals to suitable homes. A **bat** begins in a cave, while a **whale** and a **dolphin** wait in the ocean section. A **shark** blocks one route, and an **octopus** guards a shortcut. My sister draws a **squid** and a **jellyfish** to fill the empty sea spaces. On land, a **turtle** meets a **frog** beside a **snake**. Each player must explain a choice before moving. By the second round, the explanations become more interesting than who reaches the finish first.
