# 71. The Robot That Asked Questions

Our **daily** science club builds a robot for carrying library books. The **clever** design looks **convenient**, but it is **dependent** on a battery that rarely lasts long enough. We feel **confident** until the machine becomes **confused** near the stairs. That **dangerous** moment makes us stop. A **curious** younger student asks a **critical** question: why must it work alone? Her **creative** suggestion leads to a guided version that follows a marked path. The new robot does less, but it gives us more control and a clearer reason to keep experimenting.
