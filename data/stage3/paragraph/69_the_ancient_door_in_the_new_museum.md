# 69. The Ancient Door in the New Museum

Our **annual** museum trip includes an **amazing** discovery: an **ancient** door hidden behind a modern display. The guide asks an **active** group of students to compare the **actual** object with an **advanced** digital image. One **apparent** crack turns out to be part of the design. We ask for **additional** information, and she brings a drawing from storage. Our **ambitious** plan to sketch every object becomes less **appropriate**. We spend the rest of the visit studying this one door and imagining the hands that once opened it.
