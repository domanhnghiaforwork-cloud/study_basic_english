# 37. The Old Dog's Last Summer

Our **domestic** dog spends his last summer sleeping beneath a tree. I know he is **alive** when his tail moves at the sound of my voice, yet I fear the day he will be **dead**. Grandma says **birth** and **death** both belong to **life**, though that does not make loss easy. In school, we learn that **sex** and **gender** are different terms, and that **natural** changes happen over a **lifetime**. At home, the lesson is quieter: I sit beside our dog and keep him company.
