# 89. The Prize behind the Curtain

A surprise performance will **reveal** the winner of our community award. The organizer asks us not to **rush** or **separate** the guests by age. Instead, we help people **settle** at long tables and **respond** when anyone needs a seat. I am nervous because the rules **require** me to introduce the winner. My hands **shake** until I see my teacher smile. No speech can **satisfy** everyone, but this **reward** belongs to a quiet volunteer we all know. When the curtain opens, her face seems to **shine** more brightly than the stage.
