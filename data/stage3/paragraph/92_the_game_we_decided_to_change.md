# 92. The Game We Decided to Change

Our design club wants to **accelerate** a game project, but one question makes us pause: could the reward system **addict** players instead of helping them learn? We ask a mentor to **accompany** us through a test. She helps us **acquire** feedback and **act** on it. We **adopt** shorter sessions, **activate** an optional timer, and **allocate** space for clear stopping points. These changes **affect** the story, but they also **aid** younger players. Finishing later feels reasonable once we understand the kind of experience we are trying to create.
