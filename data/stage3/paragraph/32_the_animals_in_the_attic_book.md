# 32. The Animals in the Attic Book

Rain keeps us indoors, so we open an old animal book from the attic. A **donkey** and a **rabbit** share the first page with a **rat** and a **squirrel**. Farther on, a **monkey** hangs above a **gorilla**, and a **lion** faces a **tiger** across the fold. My brother likes the **leopard**, but I choose the **bear** on the cover. We decide to invent a story in which they all meet. Finding a place that suits every character turns out to be harder than naming the hero.
