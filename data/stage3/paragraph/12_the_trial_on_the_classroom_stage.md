# 12. The Trial on the Classroom Stage

For drama week, our class stages a **trial** about a fictional **crime**. I play a **lawyer**, and my friend plays the **judge**. The **court** must decide whether the person accused is really the **criminal** described by a witness. One scene mentions **prison**, but our teacher asks us to think beyond **punishment**. What would help the neighbors feel safe after **violence**? The discussion is harder than learning our lines. When the play ends, we stay behind to talk about **peace**, fairness, and the responsibility of telling a serious story.
