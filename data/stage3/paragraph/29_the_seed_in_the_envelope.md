# 29. The Seed in the Envelope

My grandmother sends a **seed** inside a letter about **agriculture**. She describes a **crop** from her childhood and the excitement of the **harvest**. I plant the **seed** beside the **grass** outside our kitchen. Soon, a **root** takes hold, then a **leaf** appears. More **leaves** follow, and one **branch** reaches toward the wall. By summer, a small **flower** opens. I send Grandma a photograph every week. She says the pictures make her feel involved in our daily life, even though her own garden is far away.
