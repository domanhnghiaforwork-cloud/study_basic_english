# 02. A Message without Its Context

A screenshot from our club chat causes a **conflict**. The **content** seems rude until someone reads the whole conversation. In **context**, the sentence is clearly a joke between old friends. We **contact** both people before making a **choice** about what to do. The real **challenge** is admitting how quickly we judged them. As a **condition** for sharing screenshots, we agree to ask first. One **benefit** is greater **confidence** in the group. Another **consequence** is quieter phones: people stop forwarding every surprising message they receive.
