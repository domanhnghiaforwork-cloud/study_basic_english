# 52. Breakfast before the Street Fair

Before the street fair opens, the **receptionist** from the nearby hotel brings coffee for the volunteers. A **secretary** checks the stall list while a **chef** and a **baker** unpack breakfast. The **waiter** and **waitress** from the cafe carry trays, and the **bartender** sets out fruit drinks. Across the road, a **butcher**, a **tailor**, and a **barber** raise their awnings. For half an hour, the street belongs to people preparing things for others. I eat a warm roll and watch an ordinary neighborhood become a festival.
