# 58. The Notice beside the Time Clock

After months of **unemployment**, Minh finds **employment** at a bicycle factory. On his first **shift**, he reads a notice about **overtime**, **insurance**, and other **benefits**. The **workplace** feels unfamiliar, but the **workforce** includes several patient people who answer his questions. Nobody promises a quick **promotion** or a large **bonus**. Instead, his supervisor gives him a clear schedule and time to learn. At the end of the week, Minh brings home a repaired bicycle frame. It is only a sample, but he cannot stop looking at it.
