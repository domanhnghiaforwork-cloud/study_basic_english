# 03. The Garden Everyone Wanted

There is growing **demand** for a garden behind our community hall. The **development** committee collects **data** about sunlight and water before making a **decision**. One **detail** surprises us: the quietest corner floods after heavy rain. That **danger** changes the design. Nobody has complete **control** over the plan, and **cooperation** becomes essential. Members from a different **culture** suggest vegetables we have never grown. The **difference** between our ideas makes the discussion lively. By spring, the empty ground holds both familiar plants and the beginning of new friendships.
