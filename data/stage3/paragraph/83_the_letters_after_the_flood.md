# 83. The Letters after the Flood

After a flood, a volunteer offers to **deliver** letters between two villages. The families **depend** on these messages to **discover** who has reached safety. Each letter helps **determine** where supplies should go next. The stories **differ**, but many describe the same fear: another storm could **destroy** what remains. At the hall, neighbors **discuss** how to **develop** a better warning system and **decrease** the danger. They **deserve** time to grieve, yet urgent work continues. I watch one woman hold a damp envelope as if it contains the first solid thing she has touched all day.
