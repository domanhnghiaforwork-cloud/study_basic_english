# 72. The Ramp at the Distant Station

A **distant** railway station becomes the subject of our **educational** film. A **disabled** traveler gives **direct** comments about the **entire** route from the entrance to the platform. We are **disappointed** to find a ramp blocked by boxes. The staff first seem **doubtful** about our project, and one interview becomes **dramatic**. Still, the most **effective** scene is quiet: a worker moves the boxes and asks the traveler to try again. The result is hopeful, although the **disappointing** delay reminds us how easily a small obstacle can change a journey.
