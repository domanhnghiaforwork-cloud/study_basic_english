# 16. Permission for the Street Fair

Our neighborhood **committee** wants to hold a street fair. We contact a city **agency** and ask which **department** handles the **application**. The **administration** sends a clear **procedure** and explains one important **regulation** about keeping the crossing open. Another **requirement** concerns cleaning the street afterward. Before adding my **signature** to a volunteer **contract**, I read it carefully with the organizer. The paperwork takes longer than making posters, but it gives us useful questions. By opening day, everyone knows where to work and who will collect the rubbish.
