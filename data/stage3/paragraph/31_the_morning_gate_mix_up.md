# 31. The Morning Gate Mix-Up

Someone leaves a gate open during our visit to the farm. A **goat** walks toward the kitchen, followed by a **pig** and a curious **sheep**. The farmer calmly guides a **cow** and her **calf** away from the road. Behind another fence, a **bull** watches without moving. A **hen** hides beneath a cart while a **rooster** calls from the roof. Then a **turkey** appears beside our teacher, and a **horse** lifts its head as if expecting an explanation. By the time every gate is closed, we all need a rest.
