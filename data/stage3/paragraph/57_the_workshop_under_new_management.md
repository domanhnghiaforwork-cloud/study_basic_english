# 57. The Workshop under New Management

The **owner** of a furniture workshop retires, leaving her former **apprentice** in charge. As its **founder**, she knows every corner, but she refuses to decide everything for him. A **chairman** from their small association introduces a retired **executive** who offers advice. An **intern** and a **trainee** help prepare the new catalogue, while a **freelancer** photographs the work. A **contractor** repairs the roof before winter. The new **employer** feels nervous, yet the workshop opens on Monday with its familiar smell of wood and a slightly different future.
