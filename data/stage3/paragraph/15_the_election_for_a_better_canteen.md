# 15. The Election for a Better Canteen

A school **election** begins with a simple question: what should change in the canteen? One **party** wants cheaper food; another wants less plastic. Each **campaign** must explain its plan before anyone can **vote**. A student **association** invites the kitchen staff to speak, and the local workers' **union** sends ideas. What began as a small **protest** becomes a wider **movement**. Our school is an **institution** where we can practice listening, says the head teacher. By the final meeting, every **organization** involved supports at least one shared proposal.
