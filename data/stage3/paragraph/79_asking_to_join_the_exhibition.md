# 79. Asking to Join the Exhibition

I **admire** the artists in our town exhibition, but I hesitate to **apply** for a place. A friend helps me **adjust** the photographs of my work and **approach** the organizer. I **admit** that I have never shown anything publicly. She says beginners may **appear** beside experienced artists if the work is ready. Before they **announce** the final list, two volunteers **advise** me on the labels and **allow** me time to revise them. I **appreciate** that chance more than an easy promise of success. For once, submitting the work feels possible.
