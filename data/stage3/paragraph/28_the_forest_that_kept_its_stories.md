# 28. The Forest That Kept Its Stories

In our adventure story, one ancient **tree** survives an **earthquake** and a **flood**. A nearby **volcano** leaves black stones after an **eruption**, while years of **drought** change the valley below. During a **freezing** winter, a child finds a rare **plant** near its roots. She wonders how each **species** has endured so much. Her grandmother says every **disaster** leaves a story, though some stories are difficult to read. The child begins drawing the forest, hoping to preserve what she can before the next season changes it again.
