# 36. The Creature with the Paper Wings

For an art project about **wildlife**, I invent a creature with soft **fur** and one bright **feather** above its **beak**. Each **wing** is made from folded paper, and its **tail** curls around a cardboard **nest**. My partner adds a small **horn** and a sharp-looking **claw**. We label it a **wild** creature from an imaginary island. Then we write a story about why its home needs protection. The teacher asks what it eats, and our beautiful model suddenly requires an entire imaginary world to support it.
