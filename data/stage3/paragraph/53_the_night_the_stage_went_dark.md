# 53. The Night the Stage Went Dark

Just before our play begins, the stage goes dark. An **electrician** checks the lights while a **mechanic** moves a noisy generator outside. A **carpenter** steadies a loose door in the set, and a **plumber** stops a leak behind the hall. The **painter** and **photographer** help carry buckets. In the dressing room, a **hairdresser** finishes the lead **actor**'s hair while a **singer** and a **musician** entertain the waiting audience. By the time the curtain rises, the story onstage has become only one part of the evening's performance.
