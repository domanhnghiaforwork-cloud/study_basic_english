# 49. The Invitation in the Drawer

An **elderly** neighbor asks me to help sort papers after her **retirement**. We find an **invitation** to a **festival**, a program from a **funeral**, and photographs from a **celebration**. Each **ceremony** brings back a different memory of her **youth**. At the bottom lies a card from her wedding **anniversary**, with a pressed flower she once received as a **gift**. We stop sorting and make tea. The papers no longer seem like clutter. They are small doors into a life I had never taken time to ask about.
