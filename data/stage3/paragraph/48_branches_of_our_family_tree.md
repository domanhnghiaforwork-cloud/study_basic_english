# 48. Branches of Our Family Tree

For a school project, I interview each relative about our family tree. One **ancestor** crossed the country by train, and a **descendant** still keeps his old ticket. My **nephew** and **niece** add drawings beside their names. Grandma chooses a photograph of every **grandchild**, while my **stepfather** shares a memory from his **childhood**. My cousin writes about her **stepmother** and the **adoption** that brought them together. As a **teenager**, I thought family history meant dates. Now the page holds journeys, choices, and people who made room for one another.
