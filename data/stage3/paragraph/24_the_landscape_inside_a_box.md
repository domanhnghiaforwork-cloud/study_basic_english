# 24. The Landscape inside a Box

For a school exhibition, we build a **valley** inside an old box. We use **soil** for the hills, **sand** for the **coast**, and a small **rock** above a paper **cave**. Blue thread becomes a **stream** flowing from a **waterfall** toward a **pond**. The hardest part is keeping the **mud** from covering everything else. When the model dries, my brother adds a tiny person beside the water. That little figure changes the whole scene. Suddenly, the landscape looks like somewhere a traveler might stop and rest.
