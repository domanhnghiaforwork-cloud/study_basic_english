# 09. When the Team Lost Its Captain

Our captain leaves before the tournament, and the **situation** brings **stress** to everyone. We need a new **system** for making decisions. One teammate suggests changing the whole **structure**; another thinks **talent** alone will carry us through. Our coach asks each person to name a **strength** and choose a reliable **source** of advice. Her **theory** is that listening can become a team skill. The **standard** is simple: no laughing at another person's **thought**. We still lose our first match, but we begin to understand how to play together.
