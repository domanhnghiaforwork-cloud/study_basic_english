# 64. The Toy Shop across the Border

In our business simulation, a toy shop wants to **import** wooden trains and **export** handmade dolls. A change in **currency** means the owner must check the **exchange** calculation again. A new **tariff** raises costs, while **competition** from another shop makes planning harder. We give each **competitor** a different **strategy**: one aims at a small **target** group, another at a broader market **segment**. Nobody wins every round. When we compare results, we find that the same decision can look sensible under one set of conditions and risky under another.
