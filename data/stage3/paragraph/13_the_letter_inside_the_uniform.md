# 13. The Letter inside the Uniform

In a fictional museum story, a girl finds a letter inside an old **military** uniform. The writer is a **soldier** waiting after a **battle**, wondering whether the **war** will ever end. He describes an **army** camp, a broken **weapon**, and his longing for his family's **safety**. The girl reads the letter during an exhibition about **security** and the **protection** of ordinary people. She adds a note beside it about human **rights**. Visitors pause longer at this small letter than at the large map of distant fighting.
