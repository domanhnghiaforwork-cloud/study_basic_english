# 80. The Debate before the Picnic

Before our picnic, we **argue** about where everyone should sit. Some **assume** that the shady tables **belong** to the first people who **arrive**. Others want to **arrange** places for older visitors. Our teacher refuses to **approve** a plan until we find a better **balance**. She asks us to **avoid** turning disagreement into a personal **attack** and to **behave** as if every guest were already listening. By noon, we have moved the tables together. Nobody owns the shade, but there is enough space for everyone to share it.
