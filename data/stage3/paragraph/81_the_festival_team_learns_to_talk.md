# 81. The Festival Team Learns to Talk

We cannot **complete** the festival plan because every meeting ends in **blame**. People **complain** about noise, **claim** the best tasks, and **bother** those trying to **concentrate**. Our organizer asks us to **communicate** through one shared notebook. We **combine** related jobs, **confirm** each deadline, and stop assuming that silence means agreement. The change feels slow until the first stall opens exactly on time. That evening, we **celebrate** with tea among the empty tables. Nobody calls the plan perfect, but we finally recognize the work behind one another's tired smiles.
