# 84. A Fair Place in the Garden

Some members **dislike** our community garden's new rules. We must **divide** the land fairly, but nobody knows how much each family needs. The organizer asks us to **examine** the unused beds and **estimate** what we can grow. We **establish** a simple system that lets newcomers **enter** without waiting a whole year. Older members **earn** extra seedlings by helping beginners, and everyone agrees to **encourage** children to join. The rules cannot **ensure** perfect weather. They can help more neighbors **enjoy** the garden, which is why we began it.
