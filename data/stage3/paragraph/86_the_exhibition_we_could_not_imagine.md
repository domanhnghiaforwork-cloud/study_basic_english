# 86. The Exhibition We Could Not Imagine

When our teacher asks us to **imagine** a neighborhood exhibition, we suggest photographs. Then a visitor helps us **identify** people whose stories rarely appear in pictures. We decide to **include** recordings and **introduce** a listening corner. To **increase** participation, we **inform** families in several languages and **involve** a local youth group. Two students **insist** on making a quiet space for older visitors. Others **lead** interviews and **maintain** the equipment. At the opening, I mention how simple our first plan was. The teacher smiles and points toward the crowded room.
