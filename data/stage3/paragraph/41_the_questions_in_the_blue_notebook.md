# 41. The Questions in the Blue Notebook

During her **recovery**, Aunt Mai keeps a blue notebook beside her bed. It contains questions about **surgery**, the **operation** she remembers only briefly, and her period of **hospitalization**. Before each visit, she writes down the **medication** and **prescription** she wants to discuss. A page about an **allergy** sits beside one mentioning an **injection** and a **vaccine** from an earlier appointment. Her **therapy** sessions have their own section. Writing helps her remember what she wants to ask, and I help by carrying the notebook when we walk together.
