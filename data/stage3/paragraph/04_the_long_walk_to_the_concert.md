# 04. The Long Walk to the Concert

Playing in the town concert has always been my **dream**. When the bus breaks down, the **distance** to the hall suddenly feels enormous. My first **difficulty** is carrying the instrument; my second is finding enough **energy** to keep walking. A stranger offers help, saying it is his **duty** to support young musicians. His kindness has a lasting **effect**. I arrive just before the **event** begins. The recording is **evidence** of my **effort**, but the **experience** I remember most is that unexpected walk with someone who believed I would arrive.
