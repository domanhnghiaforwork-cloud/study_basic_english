# 05. One Page Every Morning

I begin a new **habit**: writing one honest page before breakfast. The **fact** that nobody will read it gives me **freedom**. Some mornings, I describe a **fear** about the **future**. Other mornings, I record a small moment of **happiness**. This **feeling** of having space to think helps me **focus** at school. After a month, I notice **growth** in how clearly I understand my own choices. **Honesty** on paper is not always comfortable, but it helps me find questions I once tried to avoid.
