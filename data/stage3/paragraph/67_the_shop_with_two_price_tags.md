# 67. The Shop with Two Price Tags

A shop owner in our story keeps two windows. One shows **luxury** gifts for **wealthy** visitors, while the other offers **affordable** repairs. A **poor** student and a **rich** traveler both stop to look. The owner wants a **reasonable** return without using all her **savings**. Nearby, a **commercial** district is growing around an old **industrial** site, bringing more **competitive** prices. She cannot control every change in the neighborhood. She can choose to explain her prices clearly and keep one shelf for useful things almost anyone can afford.
