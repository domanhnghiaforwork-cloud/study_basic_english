# 87. The Experiment We Had to Repeat

Our science club wants to **prove** that a new design uses less water. We **observe** the first test carefully, but an unexpected leak can **occur** when the tube bends. Before we **publish** anything, we repeat the experiment and **provide** notes about the failure. Younger students **participate** by timing each run, while older ones **perform** the measurements. We **offer** everyone a chance to **mention** something uncertain. That honesty does not **prevent** every mistake. It does give us a clearer account of what happened and what our results can reasonably show.
