# 97. The Last Day before Graduation

Before we **graduate**, our class returns to the science fair to **explore** unfinished projects. A model balloon seems ready to **explode**, so our teacher asks everyone to **freeze** while she checks it. Nearby, an experiment shows how water can **freeze**, and a paper bridge reaches across two desks. We **greet** old partners and **guess** which ideas will **impress** visitors most. One group hopes a company will **hire** them someday; another wants to invest more time and **extend** its project. We cannot **ignore** how quickly the year has passed. Nobody feels quite ready for the room to become a memory.
