# 82. The Club That Almost Closed

When attendance falls, our reading club must **consider** whether to **continue**. The weekly meetings usually **consist** of one long talk and **contain** little time for questions. We **debate** changing the format, but several members **criticize** every suggestion. A new volunteer offers to **contribute** a short story and help us **cooperate** in smaller groups. Her enthusiasm begins to **convince** the others. We learn to **cope** with uncertainty instead of waiting for a perfect plan. By winter, arguments decrease because visitors have more chances to speak and more reasons to return.
