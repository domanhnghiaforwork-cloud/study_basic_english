# 38. Building a Model for Science Class

Our science class builds a model of an imaginary **organism**. We begin with a **cell**, then show how **tissue** can form a **muscle** or another **organ**. A cardboard **skeleton** supports the model, and yellow string marks a **nerve**. Before the exhibition, we add a separate panel about **male** and **female** animals and **reproduction**. The teacher checks our labels and asks us to explain each connection. The model looks strange, but the visitors ask thoughtful questions. I discover that making something visible can reveal what I still do not understand.
