# 33. The Missing Zoo Labels

Our class makes a pretend zoo with drawings, but several labels fall off before the exhibition. I match a **wolf** with a forest scene and put a **fox** beside a **deer**. An **elephant**, a **giraffe**, and a **zebra** belong in another display. The **camel** needs its own sandy background, while the **panda** sits near bamboo. My friend places the **kangaroo** and **koala** together on a large map. We check every label before visitors arrive. The drawings are imaginary, but we want their names to be right.
