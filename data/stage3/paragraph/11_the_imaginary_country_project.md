# 11. The Imaginary Country Project

Our class invents a **nation** for a geography exhibition. We draw a **border** around its **territory**, choose a **capital**, and design a **flag**. Next, we divide the country into a mountain **region** and a coastal **region**. The **population** must have ways to travel between them. Writing a **law** proves harder than drawing a map: what should be **legal**, and what should be **illegal**? We argue about cars in village streets until someone suggests a vote. By afternoon, our imaginary country has taught us something about living in a real one.
