# 65. The Poster That Sold Nothing

Our school shop makes an **advertisement** for reusable cups, but **consumption** barely changes. A visiting **consumer** explains that our **advertising** never shows the cup clearly. We revise the **marketing** plan and arrange a **partnership** with the art club. A local cafe offers **sponsorship** for a demonstration. After a short **negotiation**, we reach a **deal** that keeps the cups affordable. One student calls the price a **bargain**, but we ask visitors what they think. This time, listening becomes part of the project before we print another poster.
