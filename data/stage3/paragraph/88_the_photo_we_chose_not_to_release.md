# 88. The Photo We Chose Not to Release

We plan to **release** photographs from the street fair, then **realize** that some visitors never agreed to appear. The editor asks us to **reflect** before posting. We **recognize** familiar faces, but that does not remove the need to ask. Volunteers **register** requests and **remind** us which pictures require permission. Some **recommend** sharing only wide views; others **reject** that limit. We **reduce** the collection and write captions that **relate** each image to the event. The final album is shorter, yet everyone shown has helped choose how their day is remembered.
