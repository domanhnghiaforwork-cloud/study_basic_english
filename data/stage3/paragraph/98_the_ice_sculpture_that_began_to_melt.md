# 98. The Ice Sculpture That Began to Melt

Our festival team wants to **invest** in an ice sculpture, but warm weather changes the plan. We **lay** a cloth beneath the display and ask visitors not to **lean** against it. When the surface begins to **melt**, the organizer gives a small **nod** and asks everyone to **pause**. She can **persuade** the artist to **postpone** the demonstration, but the crowd must **obey** the safety signs first. We **owe** the visitors a clear explanation. Nobody tries to blame or punish the weather; we simply move the next activity indoors.
