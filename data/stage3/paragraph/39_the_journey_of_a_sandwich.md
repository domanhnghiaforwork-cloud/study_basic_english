# 39. The Journey of a Sandwich

For a comic strip, I follow a sandwich through an imaginary body. One character asks how we **digest** food, while another carries a sign about **nutrition**. Along the route, labels identify an **intestine**, a **liver**, and a **kidney**. A separate picture shows a **lung** beside an **artery** and a **vein**. I add speech bubbles for **protein** and a **vitamin**, turning each into a tiny traveler. Our teacher helps me correct the confusing parts. By the final page, the sandwich has become a reason to ask better questions.
