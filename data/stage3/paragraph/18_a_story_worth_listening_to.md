# 18. A Story Worth Listening To

A young **reporter** visits a **shelter** while preparing a story about **poverty**. Her **editor** asks for dramatic photographs, but a **volunteer** suggests spending time with the residents first. The **journalist** listens to a woman explain why a small **donation** of bus tickets matters to her family. This detail changes the article. When it reaches a wider **audience** through local **media**, a **charity** receives offers of practical help. The **reporter** returns a month later, bringing copies so the people she interviewed can read their own story.
