# 25. The Dust on the Window

In a story we read, a child lives near the **shore** and watches each **wave** through a dusty window. One morning, the **tide** brings a strange boat close to land. A strong **current** carries it past the village, leaving **smoke** above the water. The child's science notebook mentions **air**, **oxygen**, **carbon**, and **gas**, but these words cannot explain who is aboard. She wipes the **dust** away and calls her grandfather. Together, they follow the boat with their eyes until it disappears around the headland.
