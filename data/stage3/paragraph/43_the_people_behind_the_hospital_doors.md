# 43. The People behind the Hospital Doors

While waiting for my brother, I study a hospital picture book. One page shows a **surgeon** and an **ambulance**; another shows a **dentist**, a **pharmacist**, and a **therapist**. A child with a **bruise** wears a **bandage**, while a **psychologist** listens to a worried parent. The book explains that **mental** and **physical** experiences can both matter during a hospital visit. When my brother returns, he wants to look at the pictures too. We read together until Dad comes back with the car and takes us home.
