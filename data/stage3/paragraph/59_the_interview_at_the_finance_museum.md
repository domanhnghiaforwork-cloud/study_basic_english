# 59. The Interview at the Finance Museum

A museum about the **economy** advertises a part-time job. During **recruitment**, applicants bring a **resume**, one **reference**, and evidence of a relevant **qualification**. My aunt applies after receiving her **pension** because she wants to stay active. At the **interview**, she talks about explaining **financial** records to beginners. She does not claim to know every **economic** theory or every detail of **finance**. She offers patience and years of listening to people's questions. A week later, the museum asks her to help design its first workshop for families.
