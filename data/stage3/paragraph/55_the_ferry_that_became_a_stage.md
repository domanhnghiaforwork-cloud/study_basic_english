# 55. The Ferry That Became a Stage

On a charity ferry trip, a **coach** travels with an **athlete** and a **referee**. A **pilot** sits beside a bus **driver**, comparing early mornings. The **captain** asks a **sailor** to show us the safest place to stand for photographs. Near the front, a **firefighter** tells a **fisherman** about a difficult rescue, while a **hunter** quietly listens. At first, I am shy among so many strangers. Then someone starts a song, and everyone joins in. By the return journey, their job titles matter less than their voices.
