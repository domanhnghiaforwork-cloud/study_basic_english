# 17. The Notice Nobody Read

A bicycle club discovers that good **communication** requires more than one **announcement**. Its first **notice** is too small, and nobody understands the **registration** **form**. A volunteer rewrites it and adds a short **statement** explaining the ride. The group keeps a **record** of questions and prepares a **report** about the changes. When someone asks about a **license** or **permit**, the organizer directs them to the appropriate office rather than guessing. Attendance grows after the club starts listening to confusion instead of blaming people for missing the message.
