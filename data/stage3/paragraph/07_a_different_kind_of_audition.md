# 07. A Different Kind of Audition

The theater offers me an **opportunity** to audition. One **option** is a loud comic role; another is a quiet character whose **personality** resembles mine. I ask **permission** to try both. The director says the new **policy** allows this because good **performance** should not depend on guessing correctly the first time. I notice a **pattern**: **pressure** makes me rush, while **pleasure** in the scene helps me listen. My **priority** changes from winning the part to telling the story well. For once, the waiting room feels almost peaceful.
