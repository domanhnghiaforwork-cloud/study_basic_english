# 45. The Wall That Became a Mural

A blank wall outside the youth center gives our art club an unexpected project. **Curiosity** brings children to watch, and their questions become **inspiration** for the design. We need **patience** when rain washes away the first sketch, and **courage** to begin again. **Creativity** returns once everyone contributes an idea. A neighbor's **generosity** pays for paint, while her **kindness** keeps our **motivation** alive. Some visitors offer **sympathy** about the ruined work; others show **empathy** by helping us cover the new one. The finished mural belongs to far more people than its painters.
