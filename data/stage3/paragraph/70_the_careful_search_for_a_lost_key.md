# 70. The Careful Search for a Lost Key

When a museum key disappears, the **average** visitor notices nothing. The staff remain **calm** and give us a **clear** plan. A **brave** child offers to search the dark store cupboard, but the guide explains why being **careful** matters more than being bold. We become **aware** of a **basic** mistake: someone used the wrong hook. A clever volunteer checks the **available** labels and finds the key at once. Nobody is **certain** who moved it, and nobody calls anyone **careless**. We simply make the hooks easier to tell apart.
