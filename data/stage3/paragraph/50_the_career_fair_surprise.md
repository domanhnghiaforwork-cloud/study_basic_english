# 50. The Career Fair Surprise

The biggest **surprise** at our career fair is how many people work together on one building. An **architect** explains the plan, an **engineer** checks a model, and a **designer** chooses materials. A **developer** describes the software behind their work, and a **programmer** shows us a small demonstration. Nearby, an **accountant** and an **auditor** discuss records from an imaginary company. I arrived hoping to choose a **profession** by lunch. Instead, I leave with better questions about each **occupation** and the kinds of problems I enjoy solving.
