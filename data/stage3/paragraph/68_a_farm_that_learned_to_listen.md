# 68. A Farm That Learned to Listen

An **agricultural** cooperative invites an **independent** visitor to review its records. She is **professional** and careful, checking whether the figures are **accurate** before suggesting changes. The farmers want an **efficient** system, but they also want a **stable** income and a **sustainable** way to work. A **flexible** schedule helps during busy weeks, while **reliable** equipment makes each day more **productive**. The visitor's final report is short. Most of its best ideas came from people already working there, once someone made time to hear them explain their routines.
