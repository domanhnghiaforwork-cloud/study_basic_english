# 91. The App That Spoke Too Loudly

Our school app will **translate** notices, but its audio seems to **yell** when everyone else is trying to **whisper**. The developers **tend** to focus on adding features, while teachers **warn** that too much sound can **threaten** concentration. We ask them to **update** the volume controls before an **upgrade** adds more voices. A quieter design helps us **absorb** information at our own pace. The change does not **yield** dramatic headlines. It simply makes the app easier to use in the ordinary room where we need it most.
