# 75. The Honest Review of the School Play

The school paper asks me for an **honest** review of our play. My **immediate** response is that the costumes were **impressive**, but the opening was slow. I worry that this will sound unkind. At an **informal** meeting, the director tells me an **interesting** review can be both fair and **kind**. An **impatient** actor calls the audience **lazy**, yet an **innocent** question changes the discussion: was the story clear? We decide people were **likely** confused by the first scene. My review becomes more useful once I describe that problem.
