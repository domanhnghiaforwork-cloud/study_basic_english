# 60. A Long Journey for One Parcel

Our class follows a parcel from a small **marketplace** to a distant customer. The story begins with **trade** between two towns and grows into a lesson about **industry**. At the **manufacturing** stage, a delay in the **supply** of parts slows **production**. The manager wants higher **productivity**, but a **demanding** schedule causes mistakes. We redesign the **distribution** plan instead of simply asking everyone to work faster. On our map, the final **delivery** arrives a day late. The parcel still teaches us how many people stand behind one ordinary purchase.
