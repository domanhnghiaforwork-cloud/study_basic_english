# 20. Spring beside the Classroom Window

Our **classroom** has a **reputation** for being cold, so its new **status** as the garden room makes us laugh. In **spring**, seedlings fill the windowsill and change the **environment** completely. We record the **weather** and **temperature** each morning, learning that one warm day does not explain the **climate**. The **season** brings insects back to the courtyard, and **nature** becomes something we can observe between lessons. Even students who dislike gardening stop to check the plants. The smallest seedling becomes the one we all want to protect.
