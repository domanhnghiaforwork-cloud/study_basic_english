# 30. A Farmer for One Morning

Our neighbor is a **farmer** who invites our class to learn about **farming**. We arrive expecting to meet an **animal** or two, but a **dog** and a **cat** greet us before the gate opens. A **puppy** follows the **dog**, while a **kitten** watches from a basket. Near the pond, a **duck** shakes water onto a surprised **bird**. I ask whether every **animal** is a **pet**. Our neighbor smiles and tells us their names, then explains which creatures need our help before we can have breakfast.
