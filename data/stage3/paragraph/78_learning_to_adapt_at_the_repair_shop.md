# 78. Learning to Adapt at the Repair Shop

My first week at the repair shop teaches me that **quick** work is not always **precise** work. I am **proud** of finishing a clock until its hands stop again. The owner stays **quiet** while I discover a loose piece. He says a **responsible** worker takes the problem seriously, but being **serious** need not mean being afraid. With **regular** practice, I can **achieve** steadier results and **adapt** to unfamiliar tasks. The clock finally works on Friday. Its **real** lesson has taken longer than the repair itself.
