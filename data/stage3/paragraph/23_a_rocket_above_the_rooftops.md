# 23. A Rocket above the Rooftops

At **sunrise**, our school launches a paper **rocket** from the playground. The science club has decorated it with a **rainbow** and pictures of **earth** from **space**. Inside the hall, a model **satellite** hangs beside a painted **galaxy**. We spend the morning imagining a journey beyond our **planet** and into the **universe**. At **sunset**, the paper **rocket** is still caught in a tree. Nobody has traveled far, but several younger children have started asking questions. Our teacher says that can be a good beginning for any journey.
