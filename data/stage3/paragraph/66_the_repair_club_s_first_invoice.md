# 66. The Repair Club's First Invoice

The repair club receives its first **invoice** and discovers an error. A **purchase** was charged twice, so the treasurer requests a **refund**. Meanwhile, members compare a **warranty** with a written **guarantee** before buying equipment. We discuss **renting** a room instead of accepting **ownership** of an expensive workshop. A **subscription** to a tool catalogue is cancelled, but the small **membership** fee stays. After reading a story about **bankruptcy**, nobody treats the accounts as someone else's problem. The club is still small, and that makes every careful decision visible.
