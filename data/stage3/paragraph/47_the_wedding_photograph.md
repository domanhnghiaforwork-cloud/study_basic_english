# 47. The Wedding Photograph

I find a **wedding** photograph inside a book about **romance**. The **couple** look young, laughing beside an unnamed **relative**. Grandma tells me about their **marriage**, their **passion** for music, and the **affection** that made their home welcoming. Years later, a **divorce** changed their lives, but their **attachment** to the children remained. One person found a new **partner**; the other moved closer to friends. Looking again, I realize the photograph is not a promise that nothing will change. It is a record of a day they were happy together.
