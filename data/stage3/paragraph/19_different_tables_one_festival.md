# 19. Different Tables, One Festival

At our neighborhood festival, each family shares a dish and a **tradition**. One table explains a **religion**, while another displays photographs of a journey made in **faith**. We learn that **respect** does not require sharing every **belief**. Later, a discussion about **wealth** reveals how **inequality** can hide behind a cheerful event. Organizers promise to work toward **equality** by removing the entry fee. **Diversity** becomes more than a word when everyone can come. A young visitor describes **discrimination** she faced elsewhere, and the room becomes quiet enough to listen.
