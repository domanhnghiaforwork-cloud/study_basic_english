# 22. The Night the Weather Changed

The afternoon **heat** and **humidity** make everyone restless. By evening, **wind** shakes the gate, and a **storm** moves over the village. We hear **thunder** before **lightning** lights the room. Grandpa closes the curtains and tells a story about a winter with **snow**, **ice**, and **frost**. Outside, the road disappears into heavy rain. By morning, a little **fog** hangs above the fields, but the house is quiet. We step outside together to collect fallen branches and discover that the old gate has somehow stayed standing.
