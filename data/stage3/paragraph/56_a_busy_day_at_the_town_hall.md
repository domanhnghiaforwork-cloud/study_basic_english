# 56. A Busy Day at the Town Hall

At the town hall exhibition, a **guide** welcomes visitors beside a display prepared by the **librarian**. A **vet** talks to schoolchildren while a **politician** waits for a meeting with a **diplomat**. A **translator** has prepared the printed labels, and an **interpreter** helps with questions from visitors. When the sound fails, a **technician** works with the **operator** and **supervisor** to fix it. I came to photograph the exhibition. Instead, I spend the afternoon noticing the quiet work that allows so many different conversations to happen in one building.
