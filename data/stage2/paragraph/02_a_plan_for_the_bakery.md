# 02. A Plan for the Bakery

Our bakery has a new **client** who wants bread for a school festival. At our morning **meeting**, we discuss the **project** and divide the work. The **team** must follow a clear **schedule** because the bread needs to arrive before lunch. All the **staff** can help, but our **budget** is small. We cannot promise a higher **salary** yet, so nobody has to work extra hours. If this order goes well, the **business** will earn more **income**. We agree to start with a small, realistic order.
