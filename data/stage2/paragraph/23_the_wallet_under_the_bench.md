# 23. The Wallet under the Bench

While buying **clothes**, I find a **wallet** under a bench. There is a **coin** in one **pocket**, a **bill** beside a **receipt**, and a bank **card** with a name on it. I do not know whether it is a **credit** or **debit** **card**. I take everything to the service desk. Soon, a woman arrives holding an empty **purse**. She gives her name and describes the **wallet** before the assistant returns it. She thanks me warmly, and I feel glad I checked beneath the bench.
