# 01. My First Week at Work

On Monday, I start a new **job** at a small furniture **company**. The **office** is above the shop, so I can hear people moving chairs downstairs. My **boss** introduces me to the **manager** and every **employee** on our floor. One **worker** shows me how to check an order, while a **colleague** brings me tea. Later, a **customer** asks about a wooden table. I help her find the right size. For the first time, I can imagine a **career** here, learning something useful each day.
