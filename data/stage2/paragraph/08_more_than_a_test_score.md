# 08. More Than a Test Score

Before my **English** **exam**, I worry about every little **mark** on my practice paper. My teacher notices and gives me a different **test**: explain the way to the station. I speak slowly, but my partner understands. My **score** is not perfect, and my **grade** is lower than I hoped. Still, I have reached a new **level** in this **language**. The teacher says **knowledge** matters, but using it is a **skill** too. I leave the room thinking about the traveler I could help one day.
