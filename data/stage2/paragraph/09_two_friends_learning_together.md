# 09. Two Friends Learning Together

Lan helps me learn **Vietnamese**, and I help her with English. We begin each **conversation** with a **review** of yesterday's words. When I do not understand a **meaning**, she opens a **dictionary** and gives an example. I check her **spelling**, and she listens to my **pronunciation**. Sometimes our **discussion** turns to **grammar**, but we do not spend the whole evening on rules. Regular **practice** helps us speak with less fear. At the end, we order a meal using only the language we are learning.
