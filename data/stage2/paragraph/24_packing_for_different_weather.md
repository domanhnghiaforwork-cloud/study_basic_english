# 24. Packing for Different Weather

I spread my clothes across the bed before our trip. A **shirt** and a light **tee** will be useful on warm days. For cooler evenings, I choose a **blouse**, a **sweater**, and a **jacket**. Mom says a heavy **coat** is unnecessary, but rain is possible. I put **pants** and **jeans** beside my **shorts**, then add a **skirt** for our family dinner. The bag is almost full. After looking at the weather again, I take out two things and leave room for the books I want to bring.
