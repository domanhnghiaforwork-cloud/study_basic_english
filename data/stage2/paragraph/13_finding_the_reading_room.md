# 13. Finding the Reading Room

At the museum **entrance**, a guide tells us where to find the reading room. We walk **straight** through the hall and turn **left** beside a painting. In **front** of us is a tall cupboard with a map on its **side**. The room is in the **middle** of the next hall, far from the **exit**. I choose a book from the **bottom** shelf while my brother reaches for one at the **top**. This quiet **place** is exactly what we need after a noisy morning outside.
