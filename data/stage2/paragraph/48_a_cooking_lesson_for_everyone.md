# 48. A Cooking Lesson for Everyone

At our family cooking lesson, we **measure** rice and **weigh** vegetables before starting. Dad asks me to **peel** a carrot and **slice** it carefully. My sister learns to **mix** a simple sauce and **pour** it into a bowl. We **boil** the rice while Mom shows us how to **fry** small vegetable cakes. Later, we **bake** bread for tomorrow and **pack** the extra food into boxes. Nobody makes the whole meal alone. When we sit down, each of us can point to something we helped prepare.
