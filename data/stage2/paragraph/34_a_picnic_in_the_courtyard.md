# 34. A Picnic in the Courtyard

Rain changes our picnic plans, so we eat in the covered courtyard. We cut a **watermelon** and share a **peach** and a **pear**. My little brother places a **strawberry** on every plate. Mom brings **coconut** **juice**, while Dad chooses **soda**. There is bread with **butter** and **cheese**, and a bowl of **yogurt** for anyone still hungry. The food looks beautiful on our old tablecloth. We cannot sit under the trees today, but we can hear the rain and tell stories without getting wet.
