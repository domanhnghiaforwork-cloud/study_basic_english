# 21. A Tool for Our School Garden

Our class needs a tool for the school **garden**, so we go **shopping** at a farm **store**. We compare each **product** carefully instead of choosing a famous **brand**. One **item** is the right **size** but too heavy for us. Another has less **weight** and a handle that matches my **height**. The shopkeeper lets us try it in a small **field** behind the building. We choose the lighter tool and carry it back together. The next morning, even the youngest students can help us prepare the ground.
