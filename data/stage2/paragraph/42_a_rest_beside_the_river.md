# 42. A Rest beside the River

An **accident** on the cycling path ends our ride early. My brother has a small **injury**, and his knee is **hurt**. A park worker calls for help and checks whether it is an **emergency**. While we wait, my brother tries to **relax** and **breathe** slowly. I sit beside him without trying to **touch** the sore knee. We can **smell** bread from a nearby cafe but cannot **taste** it yet. Later, when an adult says he can go home, we make a quiet plan to **rest** together.
