# 29. The Little Bridge in the Picture

I am painting a bridge over a river. The paper is **smooth**, but my brush leaves **thick** lines where I want **thin** ones. I add **bright** colors to the **wide** fields and **dark** colors beneath the trees. A **narrow** path leads across the bridge toward a **heavy** wooden gate. Near the bank, the water is **shallow**; farther out, it looks **deep**. My brother asks why nobody is crossing. I paint two tiny people on the path, and suddenly the picture feels like a story.
