# 11. A Map Made of Memories

For our **geography** project, we make **art** from a map of our **country**. Each student chooses one place. I draw the **city** where I live and the small **town** where Dad grew up. Mai marks a **village** in the same **province** but a different **district**. Around the map, we add pictures of our **neighborhood**. I paint the **street** outside my home, including the tree beside the bus stop. The finished picture shows more than names. It shows the places that matter to our class.
