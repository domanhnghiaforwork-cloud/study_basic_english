# 30. Lunch after the Rain

Our clothes are **wet** after a walk, so we stop for a **meal** while they **dry**. The restaurant has a **private** room upstairs and a **public** dining area below. A **local** cook welcomes us and recommends a **dish** from her village. On one wall, a **national** food award hangs beside photos from an **international** festival. Some **foreign** visitors ask her about the recipe. She brings everyone a small bowl to try. Soon, people from different tables are talking as if they came here together.
