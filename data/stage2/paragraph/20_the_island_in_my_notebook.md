# 20. The Island in My Notebook

Our teacher asks us to invent an **island** and draw it. Mine has a **forest** below a **mountain** and a small village beside a **lake**. A **river** runs toward the **sea**, passing a green **hill** and a quiet **beach**. I add a **pool** near the school so children can learn to swim before entering the **ocean**. My friend asks where people will grow food. I draw some land behind the village and realize that making a beautiful place is easier than making one people can live in.
