# 37. Lunch at the Garden Cafe

After working in the garden, we stop at a **vegetarian** cafe. The menu offers **raw** vegetables, **boiled** potatoes, **fried** rice, and **baked** mushrooms. I choose a **tasty** bowl with several colors in it. While we wait, my sister notices soil in my **hair** and points at my **head**. I wash before eating and laugh at my reflection. Our teacher says **health** is one reason to learn about food and the **body**. For me, another reason is enjoying a good meal with friends.
