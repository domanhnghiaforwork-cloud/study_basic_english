# 45. Practicing the School Talk

We must **prepare** a short talk about a place we love. I **prefer** the river, but I **doubt** I can **describe** it well. My partner helps me **decide** which details to include. We **promise** to listen carefully and **explain** anything unclear. When I make a mistake, she asks me to **repeat** the sentence and **correct** it myself. Each practice helps me **improve** a little. By the morning of the talk, I still feel nervous, but I know the story I want to tell.
