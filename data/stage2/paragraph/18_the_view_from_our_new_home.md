# 18. The View from Our New Home

Our new **apartment** is in a tall **building** beside a busy road. From the window, I can see a **market**, a **supermarket**, and a **bank**. There is a **clinic** downstairs and a **hospital** two streets away. Dad works at a **factory** near the old **farm** outside town. On Saturday, he wants to visit the **mall**, but I would rather explore the small shops below us. Mom suggests buying vegetables at the **market** first. Soon, this unfamiliar view begins to feel like our neighborhood.
