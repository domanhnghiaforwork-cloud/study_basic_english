# 41. A Quiet Morning at the Clinic

In our school play, I act as a **patient** with a **fever** and a **cough**. My friend plays the **nurse** and asks whether I have an **appointment**. The **doctor** listens while I describe the **coldness** I felt the night before. On the desk is a pretend box of **medicine** and a large paper **pill**. Our teacher stops the scene to remind us that our play cannot tell anyone whether they have **flu**. Then we begin again, speaking clearly enough for the back row to hear.
