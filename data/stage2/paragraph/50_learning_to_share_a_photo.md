# 50. Learning to Share a Photo

Grandpa asks for **advice** about sending a photo to his sister. We **connect** his phone to the internet and **search** for the picture. I show him how to copy a short message and **paste** it below the photo. Then we **upload** the picture and **download** her reply. Before dinner, we **charge** the phone together. This small **activity** gives him a **chance** to practice without feeling rushed. His **ability** grows each time he tries. Soon, he sends another picture while I am still washing the dishes.
