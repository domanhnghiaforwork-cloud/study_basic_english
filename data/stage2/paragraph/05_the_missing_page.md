# 05. The Missing Page

Before our lesson, the teacher asks me to print a **document**. I open my **account** with a **password** and find the correct **folder**. There is only one **file** inside, but the last **page** is missing. I move the **mouse** across the **screen** and check again. Then I notice a message above the **keyboard**: the **printer** has no paper. My friend brings a new pack from the cupboard. Soon, everyone has a copy, and I can finally sit down before the lesson begins.
