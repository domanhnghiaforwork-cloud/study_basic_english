# 19. A Postcard for My Cousin

I buy a **postcard** at the **museum** and write to my cousin about our town. I describe the **park**, the old **theater**, and the new **cinema**. The picture shows a **stadium** where we watched a match last summer. Before I **post** my card, I walk past the **gym** and stop at the **pharmacy** for some soap. Outside, a **police** officer helps a child find his father. I add one more sentence to the card: the best thing here is how people help one another.
