# 03. The Bridge That Fell

Our **task** in class is to build a paper bridge. My **plan** is to fold one long sheet, but my friend has another **idea**. Our **goal** is to hold a small toy car above the table. The first bridge falls, and we laugh at our **mistake**. Then we look closely at the **problem** and find a **solution**: two folded supports. The **result** is much stronger. Our teacher calls it a **success**, but she says the first **failure** helped us learn how to build something better.
