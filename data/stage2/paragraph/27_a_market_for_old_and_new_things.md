# 27. A Market for Old and New Things

At the weekend market, Dad looks at a **silver** spoon beside a **gold** ring. They are **expensive**, so we only admire them. A **cheap** **stone** bowl catches my eye instead. The seller explains its **traditional** pattern and shows a **recent** photo of the person who made it. Nearby, a **modern** stall sells **fresh** bread. Dad buys a loaf while I carry my bowl carefully. It is not the most **valuable** thing at the market, but I know I will use it every morning.
