# 15. One Station, Many Journeys

I wait outside the **station** with my notebook. A **train** arrives above us while the **subway** runs below the street. A woman leaves her **bicycle** beside a **taxi**, and a man on a **motorcycle** stops to ask the time. Across the road, a **truck** brings boxes to a shop. Farther away, a **boat** carries people toward a large **ship** on the river. Then a **plane** passes overhead. I came here to draw one journey, but the whole town seems to be moving in different ways.
