# 10. A New Student at Sixty

At sixty, my aunt decides to continue her **education**. She visits a local **college** to ask about **training** in garden care. She does not want a **university** **degree**; she wants a useful **certificate** and time with other learners. The course includes **science**, simple **math**, and **research** in a small garden. There is also a lesson about the **history** of local farming. On her first morning, she puts a notebook in her bag and smiles. Being a student again feels strange, but it also feels exciting.
