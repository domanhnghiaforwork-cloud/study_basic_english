# 36. Grandpa's Taste Test

Grandpa sets six little bowls on the kitchen table. One contains **honey**, another **vinegar**, and another a dark **sauce**. He asks me to describe each taste: **sweet**, **sour**, **bitter**, **salty**, or **spicy**. Then he shows me how **flour** becomes a soft dough when we add water. We cook small pieces and try different toppings. Some combinations are **delicious**, and others make us laugh. Grandpa says a good cook pays attention before adding more. I write that sentence at the top of our new recipe book.
