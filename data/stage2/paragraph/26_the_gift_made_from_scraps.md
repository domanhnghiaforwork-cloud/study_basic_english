# 26. The Gift Made from Scraps

For Grandma's birthday, we want to make a gift instead of buying one. My sister suggests a **ring**, a **necklace**, or a **bracelet**. We search the craft box and find **cotton** **fabric**, a little **leather**, and some smooth **wood**. There is also a bent **metal** handle from an old **umbrella**. We avoid the sharp pieces and choose large **plastic** beads for a **necklace**. Grandma wears it at dinner and asks how we made it. Our small gift gives her a whole evening of stories.
