# 28. Choosing a Chair for Grandpa

A **famous** shop has a **popular** chair in its window, but Grandpa does not want it. He says the **complicated** frame makes him **uncomfortable**. At another shop, we find a **simple** wooden chair with a **soft** seat. A **rough** edge needs a little work, but sitting in it feels **comfortable**. The owner says this kind of chair used to be **common** and is now quite **rare**. Grandpa buys it and carries it home slowly. By evening, he is reading beside the window in his new favorite seat.
