# 38. The Face in the Mirror

For an art lesson, I draw my **face** while looking in a mirror. One **eye** is higher than the other, and my **ear** is too large. My **nose** looks fine, but my **mouth** makes me seem surprised. When I smile, I notice a small gap where a **tooth** is missing. I draw the rest of my **teeth**, then add my **tongue** as a joke. Finally, I sketch my **neck** and one **shoulder**. The picture is imperfect, but everyone knows who it is supposed to be.
