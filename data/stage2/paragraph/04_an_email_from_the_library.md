# 04. An Email from the Library

I receive an **email** about a free reading club. The **message** gives the library **address** and a **website** with more **information**. There is one **reason** I almost ignore it: I think every meeting is **online**. Then I read an **example** of a usual club afternoon. People meet in a room, choose a book, and discuss it together. Anyone without **internet** can register at the desk and read **offline**. I write back to ask about the next meeting. A week later, I walk into the library.
