# 12. The Walk to the Old Mill

The old mill stands **north** of the town **center**. To reach it, follow the main **road** until you see a narrow **lane** on the **east** side. Turn at the **corner** and take the **path** beside the river. A small **bridge** leads across the water. From there, you can see gardens to the **west** and the school to the **south**. My grandfather used to walk this way every morning. Today, he comes with me and stops often to tell stories about the people who lived here.
