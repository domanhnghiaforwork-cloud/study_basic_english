# 49. The Printer That Would Not Work

The school printer stops working before our book sale. We try to **repair** it, but one broken part needs attention. A technician explains how to **replace** it and **cleanse** the dusty outer cover with a suitable cloth. Then she shows us how to **lock** and **unlock** the paper tray. I **switch** the printer on, **press** a button, and **click** the correct file. At last, we can **print** a **copy** of our poster. The technician leaves smiling, and our sale can begin on time.
