# 46. Our Class Makes a Magazine

We want to **create** a class magazine, so our teacher helps us **organize** the work. Two students **manage** the stories while others **design** the pages. We **collect** drawings, **receive** letters from parents, and **add** a short puzzle near the back. Before we **produce** the final copies, we **remove** one story because its writer wants more time. There is still enough material to **fill** every page. When the magazine arrives, we pass it around slowly. It feels different to see our own words in print.
