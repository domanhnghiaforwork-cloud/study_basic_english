# 06. A Film for Grandma

Grandma tells me a **story** about the first cinema in her village. I want to make a short **film** about it, so I open a **video** **app** on my computer. The **software** lets me add **audio** from our conversation and some quiet **music**. Grandma chooses a **song** she remembers from childhood. We use old photos instead of actors. Our **movie** is only three minutes long, but the village **news** page shares it. That evening, Grandma receives calls from friends she has not seen for years.
