# 40. The Footprint on the Floor

A wet footprint crosses our classroom floor, and we try to guess who made it. One **foot** is smaller than the other, and a **toe** leaves a separate mark. Soon, Minh arrives with wet **feet** and explains that he stepped in a puddle. During science, we draw what lies beneath **skin**: **bone**, **blood** vessels, and the **heart**. We add the **brain** above them. Minh says the only **ache** he has is embarrassment. By lunch, the floor is dry, and nobody feels any **pain** except from laughing too much.
