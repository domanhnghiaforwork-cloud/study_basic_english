# 32. Dinner from the Market

Dad returns from the market with **beef**, **pork**, and a bag of **seafood**. My sister sees a **crab** and asks whether we are feeding the whole street. Dad laughs and puts most of the food away for tomorrow. Tonight, we cook **shrimp** and make a **salad** with **tomato** and **onion**. I wash a **potato** while my sister cuts a **carrot** into small stars. By dinner, the kitchen is warm and busy. We carry everything to the table and invite our neighbor to join us.
