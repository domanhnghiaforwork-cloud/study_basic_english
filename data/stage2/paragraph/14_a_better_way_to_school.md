# 14. A Better Way to School

Our class studies **transport** in the **area** around our school. We mark each dangerous **location** on a **map**, including a broken **traffic** **sign** near the gate. Every **direction** seems busy when lessons end. A **car** often stops across the crossing, and a large **vehicle** sometimes blocks the view. We ask whether a school **bus** could help more children travel together. The teacher sends our **map** to the school office. A week later, a worker repairs the **sign**, and parents receive a note about keeping the crossing clear.
