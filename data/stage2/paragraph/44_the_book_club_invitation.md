# 44. The Book Club Invitation

When I **return** a book, the librarian asks me to **join** a reading group. Members **share** a short story each week and **invite** someone new once a month. I want to **accept**, but I worry that I read too slowly. She says I can **refuse** to read aloud and simply listen. We may **agree** or **disagree** about a story, as long as we are kind. I **believe** her and decide to **trust** the group. On Friday, I arrive with a book I have loved for years.
