# 16. The Suitcase with a Yellow Ribbon

At the **airport**, I check my **ticket** twice before entering the **terminal**. My **passport** and **visa** are safe in a small bag, but I worry about my **luggage**. Every black **suitcase** looks like mine. Then I remember the yellow ribbon my sister tied to the handle. I find it at once and laugh. This is my first **trip** abroad and the beginning of a long **vacation**. The **journey** has only just started, but I already have a useful story to tell my sister.
