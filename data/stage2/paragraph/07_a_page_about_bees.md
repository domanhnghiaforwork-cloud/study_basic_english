# 07. A Page about Bees

In our science **class**, we read an **article** about bees. Our teacher brings a **magazine** and a **newspaper**, and we compare their pictures. The **subject** seems difficult at first, but one simple **exercise** helps: we draw a garden with places for bees to rest. For **homework**, we must find another useful fact at the **library**. This **lesson** is part of a short **course** about animals near our homes. I used to be afraid of bees. Now I want to understand them before I move away.
