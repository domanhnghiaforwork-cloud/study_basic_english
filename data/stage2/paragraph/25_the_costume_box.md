# 25. The Costume Box

Our school play needs clothes from different jobs. I open the costume box and find a **uniform**, a **suit**, and a pair of **boots**. There are **shoes** without **socks** and a **hat** with a broken ribbon. My friend chooses a **cap** and a **belt** for his character. I borrow a **scarf** and **gloves** to look like someone arriving in winter. Nothing matches perfectly, but that makes us laugh. When the teacher calls us onto the stage, the empty box becomes a seat in our pretend train.
