# 39. Drawing a Moving Person

Our art teacher asks us to draw someone throwing a ball. I begin with an **arm** and bend it at the **elbow**. Then I add a **hand**, each **finger**, and a **thumb**. My first drawing has a very long **leg** and a tiny **knee**. The **chest** is too wide, and the **stomach** seems to face the wrong way. The teacher draws a line for the **backbone** and helps me start again. This time, my person looks ready to throw instead of falling over.
