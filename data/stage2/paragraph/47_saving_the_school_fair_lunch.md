# 47. Saving the School Fair Lunch

Our class will **serve** lunch at the school fair. We **count** the guests and **compare** prices before placing an **order**. To **save** money and avoid **waste**, we ask people to choose their meals early. We **cover** the outdoor tables to **protect** them from rain. Parents **support** our plan, but a storm may force us to **cancel** the fair. On Saturday, the sky clears just before noon. We put the food outside, and soon the tables are full of people enjoying the afternoon together.
