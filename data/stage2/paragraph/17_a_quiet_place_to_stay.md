# 17. A Quiet Place to Stay

During our **holiday**, we leave a busy **hotel** and look for a quieter place. A **tourist** on our walking **tour** recommends a family **hostel** near the river. At **reception**, we ask about a **reservation** for two nights. The owner checks our **booking** and shows us the kitchen. Our favorite **facility** is a small reading room with old travel books. The **service** is friendly, and breakfast arrives in a basket each morning. By the second day, we know the other guests and feel almost at home.
