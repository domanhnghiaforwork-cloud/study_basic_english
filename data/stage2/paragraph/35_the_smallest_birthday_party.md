# 35. The Smallest Birthday Party

My cousin wants a small birthday party, so we invite just three friends. I make a **cake** with **chocolate** **cream**, and my sister puts one **cookie** on each plate. There is **candy** in a jar, but we keep it for later. Before **dessert**, Dad serves a warm **snack** made with a little **oil** and a favorite **spice**. He adds **pepper** to his own portion. We eat slowly, play cards, and sing softly when the candles come out. Nobody misses having a bigger party.
