# 43. The Playground Challenge

Our sports teacher asks us to **stretch** before the playground challenge. We **jump** over a line, **climb** a low frame, and **rise** from the ground without using our hands. If we **fall**, the soft mats help. Next, we **lift** a light ball and try not to **drop** it. I need to **borrow** a smaller one, so a friend offers to **lend** me hers. At the last station, we **pick** a ribbon from a basket. Mine says that trying together matters more than winning alone.
