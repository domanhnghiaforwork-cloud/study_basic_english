# 31. The Cafe with Two Kitchens

The **menu** at our new cafe changes every Friday. Today, one kitchen makes **soup**, **pasta**, and a **noodle** dish, while the other prepares **pizza**. My brother wants a **burger** with **sausage**, but I choose a vegetable **sandwich**. Through a window, we can watch the cook follow a handwritten **recipe**. She lets a student name one **ingredient** in each dish before serving it. We came here because we were hungry. We leave with an idea for a meal we could try making at home.
