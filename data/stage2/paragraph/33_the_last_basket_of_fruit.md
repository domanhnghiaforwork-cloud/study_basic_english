# 33. The Last Basket of Fruit

At the end of the market day, our stall has one **cucumber**, a **cabbage**, and a **mushroom** left. There is also a small bag of **bean** seeds and some **corn**. Grandma puts the vegetables in a basket for our neighbor. Then she chooses fruit for us: a **lemon**, a **lime**, a **mango**, a **pineapple**, and one last **grape**. My brother laughs at the lonely **grape**. Grandma cuts the larger fruit into pieces, and we all share a colorful snack before cleaning the empty stall.
