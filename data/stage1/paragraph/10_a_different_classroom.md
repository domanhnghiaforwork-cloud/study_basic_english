# 10. A Different Classroom

Our classroom **is** different today. There **are** pictures on the wall and books by the window. Yesterday, the room **was** empty because we **were** all outside. We **had** a day to clean and change it together. Our teacher **has** **been** at this school for many years, but she says the room feels new. "You **have** made a good place to learn," she tells us. I want to **be** the first person to read in our new book corner. My friend **is** **being** very quiet beside me. She already **has** a book open and does not want to stop.
