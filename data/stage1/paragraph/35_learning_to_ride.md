# 35. Learning to Ride

I meet Dad **early** in the morning to learn to ride a bicycle. The air is **cool**, but my hands feel **warm** on the handlebars. We find a **clean** path behind the school, away from the **dirty** road. Dad says the first part is **easy**: sit down and keep my feet ready. Moving forward is more **difficult**. I try to go **fast**, lose my balance, and stop. Dad tells me a **slow** start is better. I try again. It is **hard** work, but after a few more tries, I can ride to the end of the path.
