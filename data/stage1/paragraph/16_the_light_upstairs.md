# 16. The Light Upstairs

Our house is **near** the school, so we do not have to walk **far**. This evening, I see a light **inside** a classroom. My brother waits **outside** while I look through the window. A teacher is standing **beside** a table. She waves and asks us to come **into** the room. We carry some books **up** the stairs for her. Then she walks **down** with us and turns **off** the light. We go **out** through the main door together. She thanks us for helping. The school looks very quiet now, and we walk home before it gets dark.
