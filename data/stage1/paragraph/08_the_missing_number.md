# 08. The Missing Number

At **twelve**, our teacher puts some number cards on the table. We have to put them in order before lunch. I find **thirteen** and **fourteen** under a book. My friend has **fifteen** and **sixteen** in her hand. Another child brings **eighteen** and **nineteen** from the floor. We place **twenty** at the end, but something is wrong. There is an empty space between **sixteen** and **eighteen**. Where is **seventeen**? We look around and find it behind a chair. The clock says **twelve** **thirty** when we finish. Our teacher smiles, and we leave the cards ready for the next class.
