# 09. A Small Book Sale

I **am** at a book sale with my family. The **first** table has old books, and the **second** has new ones. At the **third** table, children can read for free. My mother gives me **fifty** **thousand** dong to spend. I choose a story for **forty** **thousand** and keep the rest. **Last** year, the school sold a **hundred** books in one day. This year, they hope to sell more. My little brother wants to come to the **next** sale with his own money. For now, he sits beside me and looks at the pictures in my book.
