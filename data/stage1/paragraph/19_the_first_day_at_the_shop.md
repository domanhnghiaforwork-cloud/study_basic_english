# 19. The First Day at the Shop

"**Hello**!" says a woman when I enter the new shop. "**Hi**," I answer. The room is **very** small, but it is **quite** busy. I **really** want a picture book for my brother. "**Maybe** you can help me," I say. "A book about a dog, **please**." She shows me one, but I drop it on the floor. "**Sorry**!" I say as I pick it up. She smiles and tells me it is okay. I buy the book and say, "**Thanks** for your help." At the door, she waves. "**Bye**! Come back when your brother finishes the story."
