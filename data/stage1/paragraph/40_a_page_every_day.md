# 40. A Page Every Day

I **study** English with my sister after dinner. She does not **teach** at a school, but she likes helping me **learn**. First, I **read** a short story. Then I **write** two sentences about it. If I do not **understand** something, I ask her to **say** it another way. We **speak** English for a few minutes before we finish. I sometimes **forget** a word I knew yesterday. My sister tells me that this is normal. "**Remember** one useful sentence and use it tomorrow," she says. A page every day is helping me feel more comfortable with English.
