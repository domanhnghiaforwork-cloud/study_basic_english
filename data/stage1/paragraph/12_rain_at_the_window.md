# 12. Rain at the Window

My brother **and** I want to go outside, **but** the sky is gray. "It **might** rain," Mom says. "**If** you go, take your coats." I look for mine **and** find it under the bed. My brother cannot find his, **so** we wait by the window. "Do you want to stay home **or** visit Grandma?" Mom asks. "**Yes**, let's visit Grandma!" I say. My brother says **no** at first **because** he wants to play with his ball. Then he remembers Grandma's dog. We are **not** going to have a boring afternoon after all.
