# 30. A Letter for Grandma

I want to send a **letter** to Grandma, but I do not know where to start. I put a piece of **paper** on the table and pick up my **pen**. Then I put it down and use a **pencil** first. I write one short **sentence** about school. There is a **word** I cannot spell, so I ask Mom a **question**. She gives me the **answer** and helps me finish. I add a **picture** of our garden and a **photo** of the family. Now Grandma can see how we are doing. I hope she writes back soon.
