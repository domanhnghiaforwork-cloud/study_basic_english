# 33. The Chair Nobody Wanted

An **old** chair stands outside our house. Dad thinks it looks **ugly** and wants to throw it away. My **young** brother has a different idea. He asks if we can give it a **new** color. Mom says it is a **good** plan. The first coat of paint looks **bad** because we use too much water. We try again, and this time the chair looks **beautiful**. Our neighbor stops to look. "What a **nice** chair!" she says. We feel **happy** when we hear her. It is **great** to make something useful from a thing nobody wanted.
