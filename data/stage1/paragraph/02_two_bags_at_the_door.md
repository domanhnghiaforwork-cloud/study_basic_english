# 02. Two Bags at the Door

**Our** family is ready to leave, but there are two blue bags by the door. "Which bag is **mine**, and which is **yours**?" I ask **my** brother. He opens one bag and shows **us** a red book. "This is **your** book," he says. I open the other bag and find **his** shoes. **Our** parents laugh at **their** two children. We put the right things into each bag and carry **them** outside. A little dog waits beside the car with **its** ball. This time, everyone knows what to take. We can finally start **our** day together.
