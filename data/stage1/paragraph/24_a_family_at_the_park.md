# 24. A Family at the Park

I go to the park with my **family** and a **friend**. Near the door, a sign says every child must stay with an **adult**. My **father** takes my hand while my **mother** carries the **baby**. My **friend** walks with his **mom** and **dad**. Their **son** is the same age as me, and we like the same games. We find a place under a tree and sit together for lunch. Another **parent** asks if there is room for her **family** too. We move our bags and welcome them. Soon, the children are playing and everyone is talking.
