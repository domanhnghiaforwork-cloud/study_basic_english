# 26. My First Night in a New Home

My **name** is Lan, and today is my **birthday**. At the **age** of ten, I have a new **home**. Our **house** is small, but there is a **room** just for me. My **bedroom** has a window near the bed. The **bathroom** is across from it, and the **kitchen** is downstairs. Some boxes are still on the **floor**, waiting for us to open them. I find my books and put them beside the window. Tonight, we eat together at a small table. There is no **birthday** cake, but Mom makes my favorite food. I feel happy here.
