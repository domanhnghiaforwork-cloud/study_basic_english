# 15. The Cat in the Garden

A small cat sits **under** a chair in our garden. Its eyes follow a bird **above** the wall. **Below** the bird, a red ball lies **between** two boxes. The cat walks **through** the open door and hides **among** the plants. Then it runs **across** the garden and jumps **over** the ball. My sister looks **around** for it, but she cannot see it. I point **behind** the big tree. The cat is there, looking at us. We bring it a bowl of water and sit down nearby. The bird stays on the wall and watches.
