# 36. Is the Reading Room Open?

A message says the reading room is **closed** today, but my friend thinks the message is **false**. "The door was **open** this morning," she says. We walk there after lunch to find out what is **true**. At first, we think we have come too **late** because the front room is **empty**. Then a woman calls us from the back. That room is **full** of children reading. "You came to the **right** place," she says. "The message had the **wrong** date." We choose two books and sit down. Reading here is **free**, so we can come back tomorrow.
