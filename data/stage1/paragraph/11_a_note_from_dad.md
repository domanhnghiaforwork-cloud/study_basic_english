# 11. A Note from Dad

Dad leaves a note on the kitchen table: "I **will** be home late. **Could** you help me this afternoon? You **should** wash the cups, and you **must** give the dog some water. You **may** play outside when you finish." I **can** **do** those things, but my little brother wants to help too. "What **does** Dad want us to **do** first?" he asks. I show him the note. He says he **would** like to wash the cups. We work together and finish before dinner. When Dad comes home, he asks, "Who **did** all this?" We both smile.
