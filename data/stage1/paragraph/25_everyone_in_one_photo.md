# 25. Everyone in One Photo

My **grandmother** wants a photo of the whole family. She sits beside my **grandfather** in the garden. My **uncle** stands behind them with his **wife**. Their **daughter**, my little **cousin**, sits on the floor in front. My **aunt** asks her **husband** to hold the baby. My **brother** and **sister** stand beside me. We all look at the phone, but nobody is ready at the same time. First, the baby starts to cry. Then my **grandfather** closes his eyes. At last, we get a good photo. Everyone is smiling, and Grandma says it is her favorite picture.
