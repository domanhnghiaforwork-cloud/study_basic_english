# 32. The New Reading Corner

Our teacher wants to make a reading corner in the classroom. We move a **big** table away from the window and put a **small** one there. A **tall** boy helps bring in a **long** shelf. We place it against the **gray** wall. The books for older children go on a **high** shelf, and the picture books stay on a **low** one. My friend brings a **purple** pillow, and I add an **orange** blanket. There is room for a **short** story before class every morning. Soon, everyone wants a turn to sit in our new corner.
