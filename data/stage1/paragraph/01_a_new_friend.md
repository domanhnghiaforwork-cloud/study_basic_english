# 01. A New Friend

**I** meet a new family at the house next to mine. A boy opens the door. **He** has a little dog, and **it** wants to play. His sister comes outside. **She** gives **me** a ball and says, "**You** can play with us." Their parents are in the kitchen. **They** make some food for everyone. **I** give the ball to the boy and ask **him** about the dog. Then **I** help his sister carry **her** bag inside. **We** sit at a small table, eat together, and talk about our school. By the end of the afternoon, **I** have two new friends.
