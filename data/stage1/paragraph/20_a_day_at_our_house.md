# 20. A Day at Our House

"**Welcome** to our house," my mother says to my cousin. He will stay with us for a **week** this **month**. In the **morning**, we eat bread and drink milk. At **noon**, Dad comes home for lunch. My cousin asks if he can help in the kitchen. "**Okay**," Dad says, and gives him some plates. In the **afternoon**, we play outside. By **evening**, we are tired, so we sit together and read. At **night**, my cousin calls his mother and tells her about his **day**. He says he already wants to visit us again.
