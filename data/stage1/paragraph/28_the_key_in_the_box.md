# 28. The Key in the Box

Dad cannot find his **key** before work. He looks in his **bag**, then under a small **box** on the table. I turn on the **lamp** because there is not much **light** in the room. The **fan** moves a piece of paper across the floor. Dad looks at the **clock** and says he is late. I move a **bottle** of water and an empty **cup**, but the **key** is not there. Then I see something in a drinking **glass** beside the **box**. It is the **key**! My little brother put it there while he was helping clean the table.
