# 13. Waiting for the Bread

Grandma has made bread **for** us **since** I was a small child. Today, I work **as** her little helper. **Before** we start, I wash my hands. She puts the bread in the oven **while** I clean the table. My brother is younger **than** me, but he wants to help too. **Although** he cannot cook yet, he can bring the plates. **After** that, we wait **until** the bread is ready. **Then** Grandma opens the oven, and the kitchen smells wonderful. We sit together **for** lunch. The bread is good, but being with Grandma is the best part.
