# 42. The Place Where We Play

The people who **live** on our street want a place for children to **play**. There is an empty room near the shop that nobody needs. We ask if we can **use** it on Saturday and **wait** for an answer. Our parents come to **work** with us, and we **start** by cleaning the floor. We **stop** for water, then **finish** putting the books and games in place. Some children **stay** to try a game while others **leave** for lunch. Before we go, we make a sign for the door. Now our street has a place where friends can meet after school.
