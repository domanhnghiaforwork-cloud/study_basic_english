# 39. Saving for Something Small

I **want** a new football, but I do not **need** one today. I still have an old ball that I **like**. Every week, I put a little money in a box instead of going to the shop to **spend** it. I **know** I must **pay** for the new ball myself. Some days, I **hate** waiting, especially when my friends have new things. Mom asks me to **think** about what I already have. I **love** playing with my friends, and our games are fun with any ball. I **hope** to buy a new one soon, but I can enjoy today too.
