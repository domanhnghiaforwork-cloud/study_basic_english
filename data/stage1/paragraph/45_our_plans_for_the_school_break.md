# 45. Our Plans for the School Break

During the school break, my friend wants to **travel** with her family. They will **fly** to another city and **visit** her uncle. I will stay near home, but I have plans too. Dad will **drive** us to a lake where we can **swim**. My brother wants to **ride** his bicycle there, but the road is too long. On Friday, we will **meet** our cousins for dinner. They love music and always ask us to **sing** and **dance** with them. I promise to **call** my friend and tell her about it. A good break does not have to take us far away.
