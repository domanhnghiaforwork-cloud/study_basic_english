# 46. Before the School Show

Before the school **show**, I **feel** so afraid that I want to **cry**. I want to **become** a singer, but standing in front of people is difficult. My teacher asks me to **check** the room. She opens the door to **show** me the people inside. "A mistake does not **mean** you are a bad singer," she says. I take a slow breath and try to **smile**. Things do not **seem** so frightening now. Whatever may **happen**, my friends will help me. I walk onto the stage. In the first song, a funny line makes everyone **laugh**, and I relax.
