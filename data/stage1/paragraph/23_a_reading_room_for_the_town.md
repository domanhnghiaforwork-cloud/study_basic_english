# 23. A Reading Room for the Town

In **October**, a **man** puts a box of books outside his house. A **woman** stops to look and takes a story home. The next day, she brings it back with two more books. By **November**, many **people** are sharing books there. One **person** brings a small table, and a **boy** helps carry some chairs. A **girl** makes a picture for the door. In **December**, the town has a little reading room. Every **child** is welcome to come and read for free. There is no big television or computer, just books, chairs, and **people** who enjoy a good story.
