# 21. A Little Time to Read

This **year**, I want to read a little every day. I do not need a whole **hour**. Even one **minute** is a good start when I am busy. On **Monday**, I put a book beside my bed. On **Tuesday**, I read a page before breakfast. By **Wednesday**, I want to know what happens next. On **Thursday**, I take the book to school. A friend reads with me on **Friday**. We laugh at a funny **moment** in the story. Now I make **time** to read because I enjoy it. A small start can become something important.
