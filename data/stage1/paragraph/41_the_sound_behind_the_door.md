# 41. The Sound Behind the Door

My sister and I **hear** a strange sound behind the kitchen door. We stop to **listen**. It sounds like a small animal. "Can you **see** anything?" she asks. I **look** through the window, but the room is dark. We **ask** Dad to **help** us. He opens the door and tells us to **watch** the floor. A little cat walks out from under a chair. We **try** to call it, but it runs away. Later, we **talk** to our neighbor, who is looking for her cat. We **tell** her what happened and show her where it went.
