# 48. Breakfast at Grandmas House

On Sunday, we have breakfast at Grandma's house. She puts **an** old blue cup beside my plate and fills it with **milk**. Dad has **coffee**, and Mom asks for **tea**. There is **bread** on **the** table, with some other **food** in little bowls. Grandma brings **the** **rice** from **the** kitchen while I pour **water** for everyone. We eat slowly and talk about our week. **A** bird sings outside **the** open window. When **the** meal is over, I help wash **the** cups. Grandma says I can come back next Sunday, and I already know I will.
