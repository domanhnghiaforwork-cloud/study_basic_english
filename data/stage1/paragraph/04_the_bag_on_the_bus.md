# 04. The Bag on the Bus

**Someone** leaves a small bag on the bus. "**Whose** bag is this?" the driver asks. **Nobody** answers. He looks inside and finds **something** useful: a book with a name on it. "Does **anyone** know a girl called Mai?" he asks. **Everyone** looks around, but she is not there. **Which** school does she go to? The book gives him the answer. There is **nothing** else in the bag except a pen. He does not take **anything** out. At the next stop, he calls the school. Mai comes with her mother, checks **everything**, and thanks him for his help.
