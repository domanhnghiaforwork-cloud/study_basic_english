# 05. Food for Everyone

**All** our friends come to the house for lunch. We have **many** apples, but only a **few** cups. There is **some** tea in the kitchen and a **little** milk in the bottle. "Do we have **any** bread?" my sister asks. We do, but there is not **much** left. I cut it into small pieces so everyone can have **some**. **Most** people want tea, so we need **more** water. My father brings another bottle from the kitchen. We put **less** sugar in the tea this time. Everyone likes the food, and nobody leaves the table hungry.
