# 49. Shopping for Dinner

Mom and I go shopping for dinner. She wants **chicken**, but I would like **fish**. We decide to buy a little of each and leave the other **meat** for another day. At the **fruit** shop, I choose an **apple** for myself and a **banana** for my brother. Mom asks me to find a green **vegetable** for the meal. Back at home, I put an **egg** in a bowl while she starts to cook. She adds a little **salt**, then asks me to make tea. I almost put **sugar** into the wrong bowl. We laugh, and I check before adding anything else.
