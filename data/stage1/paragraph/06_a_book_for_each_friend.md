# 06. A Book for Each Friend

**Every** Friday, two friends visit our little book room. They **both** like stories, but they do not always choose the **same** book. Today, **each** friend can take **one** book home. **One** boy wants a story about a dog. His friend wants **another** story, about a family. There are two books on the table, but **neither** is right for them. I show them some **other** books by the window. "You can choose **either** of these," I say. Now there are **enough** books for everyone. They sit down to read before they make their choice.
