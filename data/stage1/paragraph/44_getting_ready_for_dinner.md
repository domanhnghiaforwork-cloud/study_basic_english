# 44. Getting Ready for Dinner

My sister and I want to **cook** dinner before our parents come home. First, we **wash** our hands and **find** the food in the kitchen. I **wear** an old shirt because I might get dirty. My sister helps our little brother **dress** before he sits down to watch us. We **turn** on the light above the table and **keep** a bowl nearby for the vegetables. "**Hold** this for me," she says as she gets a spoon. We **change** our plan when we cannot **find** any rice. Bread and eggs will be fine. The important thing is not to **lose** time arguing.
