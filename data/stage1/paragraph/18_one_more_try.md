# 18. One More Try

I **often** read beside my little sister after dinner. **Sometimes** she asks me to read the same story **again**. She **never** gets tired of the little dog in the book. Tonight, she wants to read it herself. She **already** knows the first page, but the next one is **still** difficult. I have **just** sat down when she asks for help. "**Only** one word," she says. I show her how to say it. She **also** reads the last page, then smiles at me. I am happy **too**. Tomorrow, she wants to try a different book.
