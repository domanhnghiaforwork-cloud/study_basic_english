# 38. A Small Sale for the School

Our class wants to **buy** new books for the school. We decide to **make** little picture cards and **sell** them on Saturday. The teacher asks each child to **bring** some paper and **put** it on the big table. We work together after class. I **send** a message to my parents about the sale. "**Come** early if you want to **get** a card," I write. On Saturday, people **take** their time looking at our pictures. Some **give** us extra money to help. By noon, almost every card is gone. We are excited to choose the new books.
