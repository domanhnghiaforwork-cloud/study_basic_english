# 34. A Long Walk Home

After school, I walk home with my brother on a **hot** afternoon. We are **tired** and **hungry**, and my brother says he is **thirsty** too. Then we hear a little dog behind a wall. It looks **sad** and **afraid**. My brother is **angry** because someone has left it there without water. I tell him to stay calm and call Mom. She brings water and takes the dog to a doctor. It is **sick**, but it can get better with care. A few days later, we visit again. The dog looks **healthy** now, and its nose feels **cold** against my hand.
