# 14. A Letter from Home

A letter **from** my sister is **on** the table. She is away **at** school and writes **to** me every week. I sit **in** the kitchen **with** a cup **of** tea and open it. She tells me **about** her new friends and a little shop **by** the school. **At** the end **of** the letter, she asks for a picture **of** our dog. I try **to** take one, but he will not sit **without** his ball. I give it **to** him, and he finally stays still. Now I have a photo **to** send back **to** her.
