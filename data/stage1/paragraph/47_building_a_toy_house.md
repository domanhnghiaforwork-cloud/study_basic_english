# 47. Building a Toy House

Dad helps us **build** a toy house from a box. First, we **choose** a place on the table and **carry** the box there. Dad uses a knife to **cut** out a little door. I **push** it open while my brother holds the wall. "Do not **pull** too hard, or you will **break** it," Dad says. We make a small window too. My brother drops the roof, but we can **fix** it with some paper. We **throw** the extra pieces into a bag. One piece falls past the bag, and I **catch** it. Soon, our little toy family has a home.
