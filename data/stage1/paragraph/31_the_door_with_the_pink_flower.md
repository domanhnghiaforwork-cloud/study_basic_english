# 31. The Door with the Pink Flower

My friend tells me the **number** of her house, but I forget it on the way. I remember the **color** of the door: **blue**, with a **pink** flower on it. I walk past a **red** house with a **green** gate. The next house has a **yellow** wall and a **black** door. A **white** cat sleeps on its **brown** chair. Then I see the flower I remember. My friend opens the door before I can call her. "You found us!" she says. I laugh and ask her to write the house **number** on a piece of paper for next time.
