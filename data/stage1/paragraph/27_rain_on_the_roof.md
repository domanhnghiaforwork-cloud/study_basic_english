# 27. Rain on the Roof

Rain falls on the **roof**, and the garden grows dark. I close the **window** beside my **bed** and pull the **blanket** over my feet. My book is on a **chair**, just beyond my hand. I do not want to leave my warm **pillow**, but I get up anyway. Through the open **door**, I see Dad sitting on the **sofa**. Mom is at the **table** with a cup of tea. A family picture hangs on the **wall** behind her. I carry my book into the room and sit between them. The rain keeps falling, but our house feels warm.
