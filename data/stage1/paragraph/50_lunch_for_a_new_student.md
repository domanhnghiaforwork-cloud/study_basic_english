# 50. Lunch for a New Student

A new **student** comes to our **school** today. At **breakfast**, Mom gives me some **money** for **lunch**. She works at a small **restaurant** next to the book **shop**. At noon, I see the new boy sitting alone. He has forgotten his food, so I ask him to come with me. Mom shows us a meal with a low **price**. We have enough **money** to share it. Our **teacher** comes in for her own **lunch** and sits nearby. After **school**, the boy thanks me and asks if we can play together before **dinner**. I think I have made a new friend.
