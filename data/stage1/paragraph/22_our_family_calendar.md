# 22. Our Family Calendar

On **Saturday**, we make a family calendar. My brother draws a picture for **January**, and I write our plans for **February**. In **March**, we want to visit Grandma. Her birthday is in **April**. We have no family visits planned for May. School finishes in **June**, and we hope to travel in **July**. **August** is usually a quiet month at home. In **September**, we go back to school with new books. We leave some empty spaces for plans we do not know yet. On **Sunday**, we show the calendar to our parents and put it on the kitchen wall.
