# 07. Counting Before the Game

**Two** children wait by the school door. **Three** more children come, so now there are **five**. The teacher brings **four** balls, and the children help her carry them outside. **Six** parents sit near the wall to watch. We make a game with numbers: I say **seven**, and everyone takes **seven** small steps. Then I say **eight**, and we all clap **eight** times. The next numbers are **nine** and **ten**. By **eleven** in the morning, we are tired and happy. We sit under a tree, drink some water, and ask the teacher if we can play again tomorrow.
