# 17. A Visit from My Brother

My brother lives far **away**, but he is **back** at home **today**. **Yesterday**, he called and told us he was coming. **Now** his bag is **here** by the door, and everyone is happy to see him. He **usually** brings us a little gift. This time, he has a book for me and some tea for Mom. "Is Dad **there** in the kitchen?" he asks. Dad comes out and gives him a big smile. We **always** eat together when my brother visits. **Tomorrow**, we will go to the park, but tonight we just want to talk.
