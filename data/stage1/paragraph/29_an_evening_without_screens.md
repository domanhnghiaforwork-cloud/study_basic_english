# 29. An Evening Without Screens

Tonight, we put the **phone** and **computer** away before dinner. Dad turns off the **television**, but Mom leaves the **radio** on quietly. My brother brings a **plate** for each person, and I carry a **bowl** of rice to the table. Grandma asks for a **spoon**. Dad uses a **knife** to cut the bread, and my brother drops his **fork**. We laugh and talk about our day. After dinner, Mom opens a **book** and reads a story to us. Nobody asks to watch anything. It feels good to listen to a real voice in the room.
