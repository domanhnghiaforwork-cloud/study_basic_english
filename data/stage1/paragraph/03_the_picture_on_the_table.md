# 03. The Picture on the Table

**This** morning, I find an old picture on the kitchen table. "**Who** is **that** boy?" I ask my mother. "Your father," she says. "**What** is he doing, and **where** is he?" She tells me he is playing near his old house. "**When** did you take the picture?" I ask. She says it was before I was born. **These** questions make her smile. She brings out another box. "**Why** do you keep all **those** pictures?" I ask. "They help me remember," she says. Now I understand **how** a small picture can hold a big story.
